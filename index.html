Privacy & Security Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: March 2026 | Version 2.4 Enterprise/Educational

1. Executive Summary & Client-Side Execution Guarantee

Welcome to HASSAN ASCII - BINARY (hosted at binary-maker.vercel.app). We deeply value your digital privacy and recognize the importance of secure data handling. This comprehensive Privacy Policy governs your utilization of our web application.

Absolute Local Guarantee: Our application architecture is engineered around 100% client-side execution. All conversions (text to ASCII, ASCII to binary, and step-by-step division math ladders) occur entirely within your local browser runtime via JavaScript. No keystrokes, character strings, or calculated payloads are ever transmitted across networks to external remote servers, cloud logs, or third-party databases.

2. Data Collection and Processing Scope

To ensure complete transparency regarding what data touches our platform, we maintain a strict Zero Data Retention policy:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, telephone numbers, physical addresses, or financial records.
  • Input Content Security: Whether you type uppercase text, lowercase alphabets, numeric digits, complex programming symbols, or custom ASCII codes, your typed characters remain exclusively on your device screen.
  • Analytics & Telemetry: We do not deploy invasive third-party tracking scripts, user profiling pixels, or behavior monitoring tools that compromise your browsing confidentiality.

3. Cookies, Cache, and Browser LocalStorage

HASSAN ASCII - BINARY rejects intrusive marketing cookies. However, for optimal user experience and seamless UI responsiveness, the application may utilize standard HTML5 browser LocalStorage.

This local storage is restricted strictly to saving user-selected display preferences (such as your 8-Bit Padding toggle state, audio feedback settings, or theme configurations). This data never leaves your device and can be erased instantly by clearing your browser cache.

4. Educational Integrity and Mathematical Accuracy

Our platform is crafted specifically as an educational aid for computer science students, educators, programmers, and technology enthusiasts who wish to visualize successive binary division ("Tajzee/Division Method") and ASCII numerical mapping.

The application is hosted via modern cloud infrastructure (Vercel). While Vercel provides secure SSL/TLS encryption for delivering static frontend assets to your browser, they do not intercept or inspect the internal mathematical operations running in your client session.

If any third-party hyperlinks appear within auxiliary educational references, we bear no liability for the external privacy practices or terms of service administered by those third-party domains.
